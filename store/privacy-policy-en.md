# Privacy policy

CleanYourTV launcher (package `com.gathin.tvhome`).

Publisher: Martin Pohl.
Contact: https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv

Effective date: 13 November 2026.

This policy covers this launcher only. A separate phone app that talks to a TV over the network is not this app and is not described here.

## Short version

The launcher does not collect personal data and does not send data off the TV. It has no internet permission, no account, no advertising and no analytics. Nothing is sold or shared. There is no server, so there is nowhere for the data to be received.

## What the launcher accesses on the TV

- Apps that can appear on a home screen, so it can draw tiles. The list stays on the TV.
- The Wi-Fi network name, only if you agree, to label the network button. It stays on the TV. On Android 12 and older, Android requires a location permission for that read. The launcher does not use GPS and does not share a location. On Android 13 and newer it uses the nearby Wi-Fi devices permission instead, and that permission is declared not to be used for location.
- Whether the link is Wi-Fi, Ethernet or offline, to label the same button. This is not sent anywhere.

## Sharing

No personal data is sold or shared with anyone. There are no advertising or analytics partners.

## Retention and deletion

Consent and layout choices stay in the app’s private storage on the TV until you clear that storage or uninstall the launcher. That deletes them. There is no account to delete.

## Store listing

The same text is shown in the app under Launcher settings → Privacy policy. The public URL of this page, once you host it, is the URL to paste into Play Console. Do not point the listing at the privacy policy of the separate phone app.
